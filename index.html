Guide

Fitness, food & fasting tips

Intermittent fasting

Month 1 — 16:8: Fast for 16 hours, eat within an 8-hour window (11am–7pm). Drink water, black coffee, or plain green tea during fasting hours. Break your fast gently — don't binge.
Month 2+ — 18:6: Narrow your eating window to 6 hours (12pm–6pm). Your body is now fat-adapted and this shift will accelerate results.
Fasted morning runs are your superpower. Running before breaking your fast forces your body to burn stored fat. Just water before you head out.
Break your fast with a light protein meal — 2 boiled eggs, or idli with sambar. Avoid heavy carbs as your first meal.

Running program

Week 1–4: Run/walk intervals. Do not skip the walk breaks — they are part of training, not a sign of weakness. Focus on finishing, not pace.
Week 5–8: Continuous running. If you can't complete a session, repeat the previous week. Progress over perfection.
Week 9–12: Building to 10K. Run at a conversational pace — you should be able to speak short sentences while running.
Recovery days matter. Your muscles repair and grow stronger on rest days. Light walking on rest days is encouraged.
Hydrate well after every run. Rehydrate with water and electrolytes (coconut water or neer moru are ideal).

South Indian diet guide

Best breakfast choices: Idli (steamed, low fat), pesarattu (high protein), oats upma, boiled eggs. Avoid heavy ghee-fried items in the morning.
Best lunch/dinner: Sambar rice (brown rice preferred), dal with chapati, rasam, aviyal with rice. Keep portions moderate — one cup of rice maximum.
High-protein picks: Grilled chicken breast, boiled eggs, egg curry, fish curry, chana masala. Aim for 100–130g protein per day.
Avoid or limit: Biryani, deep-fried snacks (bhajis, vadas), sweets (halwa, payasam), white rice in large portions, sugary drinks including packaged juices.
Smart swaps: Brown rice → white rice · Grilled chicken → fried chicken · Neer moru → soda · Fruit → biscuits/sweets as snacks.
Eating out: Choose tandoori or grilled items, dal, rasam-based dishes. Avoid buffets. Request less oil and no butter naan.

Hydration

4L daily target for your size and activity level. Start with 500ml water as soon as you wake up, before your run.
During fasting hours: Water, black coffee (no sugar), and green tea are all fine and do not break your fast.
Signs of dehydration: Dark yellow urine, headaches, fatigue, hunger (thirst is often mistaken for hunger). Drink water first.

General tips

Sleep 7–8 hours. Poor sleep raises cortisol (stress hormone) and directly causes fat retention around the belly. Sleep is training.
Weigh yourself daily at the same time (morning, after bathroom, before food). Daily fluctuations of 0.5–1kg are normal — focus on the weekly trend.
A caloric deficit of 500–700 kcal/day equals roughly 0.5–0.7kg of fat loss per week. Combined with your runs, 15kg in 90 days is very achievable.
Don't fear a bad day. One bad day does not erase a week of effort. Log it honestly and keep going. The streak will rebuild.
