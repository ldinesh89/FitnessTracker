🕐 Fasting guide (16:8)
Your eating window is 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM every day.
8:00 PM – 12:00 PM
Fasting window. Water only. Black tea or black coffee without sugar are fine.
12:00 PM – Meal 1
Break your fast with a proper South Indian lunch. Chicken or egg based. 1 cup of rice. Sambar or rasam. Vegetables.
3:00–4:00 PM – Meal 2
Optional high-protein snack. Two boiled eggs, grilled chicken strips, or curd. Skip if not hungry.
7:00–8:00 PM – Meal 3
Final meal of the day. 1 cup rice maximum. Protein focus. Light on ghee and oil. Done by 8 PM strictly.
After 8:00 PM
Fasting begins again. Water only. Prepare for tomorrow.
🍳 Diet rules for your profile
Built for a 37-year-old sedentary male at 109 kg targeting 15 kg loss.
Daily calorie target
1,800 to 1,950 kcal. Creates a 500 to 700 kcal daily deficit from your estimated TDEE of 2,450 kcal.
Protein target
At least 120 g protein per day from chicken breast, eggs, curd, and legumes.
Rice portion
Maximum 1 cup per meal, maximum 2 cups total per day. Stick to this consistently.
Ghee and peanut oil
Use in small, measured amounts. Each teaspoon of ghee is 45 kcal. It adds up fast.
Water
Minimum 3 litres per day. Already your habit, keep it up.
